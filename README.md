# Hey, I’m part of **Craft Setu** 👋

### A Class 8 student startup for makers

**Craft Setu** is a handmade-craft community platform built by a group of **Class 8 students**.  
We believe every stitch, fold, and glaze tells a story — and those stories deserve a home.

🌐 **Live site:** [craftsetu.github.io](https://craftsetu.github.io/)

---

## ✨ What is Craft Setu?

A warm space where people can:

- 📷 **Upload** photos of their crafts  
- ❤️ **Like** and explore others’ work  
- 🧵 Discover ideas across crochet, pottery, macramé, embroidery, resin art & more  
- 🌱 Learn and grow together as a community
