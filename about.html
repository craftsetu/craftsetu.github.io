Built by Makers, for Makers

A Class 8 student startup — artisans, organizers, and technologists building the bridge between tradition and today’s creators.

Our story

Craft Setu is a student startup — built by a group of Class 8 makers who believe every handmade piece carries a story worth sharing. We set out to create a warm, welcoming home where people of every skill level can learn, post their work, and cheer each other on.

From pottery and macramé to embroidery and resin art, we’re connecting creativity across communities — one stitch, fold, and glaze at a time. What started as a classroom idea is growing into a platform for makers everywhere.

“Every stitch, every fold, every glaze tells a story — we’re here to help those stories find their people.”

— The Craft Setu team
